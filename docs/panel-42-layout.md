# 4.2" Panel Layout

*Introduced in v0.7.0. 4.2" panel only (`panel-42` env).*

The 4.2" panel no longer mirrors a subset of the 5.79" screen. It has its own
German-language layout, designed around a readable forecast plus the family
calendar. It is implemented in `src/screen_42.h` (`draw_layout_42()`), called
from `display_main_screen()` in the `#else` branch of the `PANEL_579` guard.

Dropped relative to the 5.79" panel: Aare water temperature, pollen, UV index,
Withings weight, and the fun fact.

## Regions (400 x 300)

| y | Content |
|---|---|
| 0..159 | Calendar: full date header, then today's and tomorrow's events |
| 166 | Divider (17px clearance below, 7px above in the 6-event worst case) |
| 183..296 | Weather: big temperature, condition icon, detail column, two forecast entries |

```
 Montag, 17. August 2026
 Heute   ganzt.  Termin ganzer Tag
         08:00   Spülung Abwasserleitung
         18:30   Elterncafé Plus!
 Morgen  ganzt.  Ferienbeginn
         10:00   Turnen Kinder
─────────────────────────────────────────────
                   ☀           Heute   18°/31°
    26°          (icon)        Wind     7 km/h
                               Regen       30%

  In 2h:  22°            Morgen:  23°
  🌧 leichter Regen      🌧 Regen
```

### Calendar

Day labels sit in a left gutter (x=10) so that adding the "Morgen" section
costs no extra rows. Columns: gutter (font 12), time at x=72, title at x=130.
Rows are 20px apart starting at y=40.

The renderer caps each day at **3 events**, so the worst case is 6 rows and the
band cannot overflow even if the calendar API's `max_events` is raised. When
today has no events, a single `Heute — keine Termine` row is shown; the
tomorrow section is omitted entirely when tomorrow is empty.

Titles are truncated with a trailing `.` by `s42_fit()` when too wide.

### Weather

The big temperature uses `logisoso62` (size 78, digits only). u8g2 anchors text
on the baseline at `y + size`, and that font is 62px tall for a nominal size of
78, so the `y` passed in sits ~16px above the visible top of the digits — this
is why the temperature is drawn at y=162 but appears to start at ~178.

Condition icons come from `include/weather_icons.h`, generated by
`tools/gen_weather_icons.py`. They are 1bpp, **0 = ink** (matching
`EPD_ShowPicture`), and widths are a multiple of 8 because `EPD_ShowPicture`
advances 8 pixels per byte without a bounds check inside the byte loop.

## Previewing

`sim/` compiles this exact header on the host:

```bash
cd sim && make venv   # once, if python3 lacks Pillow
make open
```

Edit `sim/data_stub.h` to preview other states (no events, 3+3 events, negative
temperatures, long titles).
